05 / METHODS

Sources and calculations

View October archive ↗

The base Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1 weights four categories equally. Gemini composite scores and costs may use the newer v5.0.2 suite; see each model’s version and date. ModelScope shows published figures.

  • Knowledge work · 25% AA-Briefcase v1.1 (15%), AutomationBench-AA (5%), GDP.pdf (5%)
  • Coding · 25% FrontierCode-AA 2.0 (15%), Terminal-Bench 4.0 (10%)
  • Science · 25% Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 (15%), CritPt v2 Hard Subset (10%)
  • General · 25% AA-Omniscience v1.1 accuracy (10%) and non-hallucination (5%), HLE-Diamond (10%)

Cost per Intelligence Index task is Artificial Analysis’s weighted average of benchmark costs. Each benchmark cost reflects input, cache hit, cache write, reasoning, and answer token prices divided by its task count. This cost stays on the X-axis when you change the Y-axis benchmark.

The Pareto calculation uses the shown families and published values. A configuration is on the frontier if no other eligible configuration scores at least as high and costs at most as much, with a strict improvement in either measure. Zoom changes the viewport. A missing cost leaves the model in the table, and a missing score leaves that benchmark blank.
